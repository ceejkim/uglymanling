# Rekkoe Vision Document

## 1. Overview

Rekkoe is a platform that unlocks local knowledge through real-time conversations with people who actually live it. It enables users to discover, learn, and experience places through direct access to locals—whether for everyday activities, niche interests, or highly personalized experiences.

Rekkoe is not just about travel—it's about access. Access to people, insight, and experiences that are otherwise hidden behind social circles, local familiarity, or expertise.

---

## 2. Mission

To democratize access to local knowledge and human expertise by making it easy to connect, chat, and learn directly from people who know best.

---

## 3. Vision

Rekkoe aims to become the default interface for discovering how to experience the world—through people, not platforms.

Instead of searching for answers, users talk to someone who already knows. Whether it's finding the best tacos, discovering where to play pickleball, crafting the perfect morning, or training with a high-level athlete, Rekkoe turns knowledge into connection and connection into experience.

---

## 4. Core Principles

### 4.1 Conversation is the Product

* Chat is the primary interface
* Real-time, human-driven discovery
* Context-rich, personalized answers

### 4.2 Local Knowledge as Infrastructure

* Knowledge lives in people, not databases
* Surface insights that are dynamic and evolving
* Unlock access to what isn't searchable

### 4.3 Experiences as an Extension of Conversation

* Experiences emerge naturally from chat
* Can be guided (local joins) or self-guided (local designs it)
* Flexible, personalized, and situational

### 4.4 Access to People, Not Just Places

* Connect with locals, enthusiasts, and professionals
* Enable learning, training, and mentorship
* Expand beyond geography into expertise

### 4.5 Authenticity & Trust

* Verified identities and reputation systems
* Transparent feedback loops
* Emphasis on genuine, high-quality interactions

---

## 5. Target Users

### Explorers

* People looking to do something new, better, or different
* Curious individuals seeking authentic, non-generic experiences
* Not limited to travelers—includes locals exploring their own city

### Locals & Knowledge Holders

* People with strong local context or niche expertise
* Enthusiasts (foodies, athletes, hobbyists, insiders)
* Professionals offering training, coaching, or guided sessions

---

## 6. Key Capabilities

### 6.1 Chat with Locals

* Real-time messaging as the core interaction
* Ask anything—from simple recommendations to detailed planning
* Build trust and context through conversation

### 6.2 Dynamic Experience Creation

* Experiences form organically from chat
* Guided: local participates in the experience
* Self-guided: local designs a plan or playbook

### 6.3 Everyday + Niche Discovery

* Find what's actually worth doing right now
* Examples:

  * Where to play pickleball
  * Best taco spots
  * Designing the perfect morning
  * Hidden or hyper-local activities

### 6.4 Access to Expertise

* Connect with people who have real skill or experience
* Examples:

  * Training with a college athlete
  * Learning from a local chef
  * Getting coached or guided in a specific activity

### 6.5 Seamless Transactions

* Book experiences directly through the platform
* Flexible pricing based on interaction type
* Secure payments and scheduling

---

## 7. Differentiation

* Chat-first, not listing-first
* Focus on people as the source of truth
* Blends discovery, social connection, and marketplace
* Supports both casual recommendations and high-value expertise

---

## 8. Long-Term Vision

Rekkoe becomes a global layer of human knowledge where:

* Every question can be answered by the right person
* Every activity can be enhanced by local or expert insight
* People monetize what they know, not just what they do

Over time, Rekkoe evolves into a network where access to people replaces traditional search and static discovery.

---

## 9. Success Metrics

* Volume and quality of conversations
* Conversion from chat to experience or transaction
* Repeat engagement across different contexts
* Earnings and retention of locals and experts

---

## 10. Future Opportunities

* AI-assisted matching between users and locals/experts
* Real-time translation for global accessibility
* Hybrid AI + human recommendations
* Expansion into learning, coaching, and micro-services

---

## 11. Summary

Rekkoe transforms discovery into conversation—unlocking a world where the best experiences, insights, and opportunities come directly from people, not platforms.

---

## 12. How to Use This Document

**This vision serves as the north star for all development decisions.**

### For Feature Development
Before building any feature, ask:
- Does this strengthen the core insight that "conversation is the product"?
- Does it deepen access to local knowledge or expertise?
- Does it bring explorers and locals closer together?

### For Design & UX
- Prioritize chat and conversation clarity
- Build trust and authenticity into every interaction
- Make real-time human connection frictionless

### For Product Roadmap
- Align sprints with the core capabilities (sections 6.1-6.5)
- Measure success against the metrics in section 9
- Use section 10 as inspiration for extensions

### For Team Communication
- Reference this document when resolving trade-offs
- Use it to onboard new team members on strategic intent
- Keep it updated as your understanding evolves
