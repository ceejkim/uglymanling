# Rekkoe Vision

## 1. Vision Statement
Rekkoe is the chat-first platform for discovering what to do, how to do it, and who to learn from through real people with real local knowledge.

## 2. Mission
Democratize access to local insight and practical expertise by connecting explorers directly with locals, guides, and professionals.

## 3. Product North Star
`Conversation is the product.`

If a feature does not improve conversation quality, trust, or conversion from chat to experience, it is out of scope.

## 4. Core Product Principles
1. Chat first, listings second.
2. Knowledge lives in people, not static databases.
3. Every conversation should be able to become an actionable experience.
4. Trust and authenticity are product requirements, not add-ons.
5. Discovery should support everyday moments and high-value expertise.

## 5. Primary Users
1. Explorers:
   - people seeking authentic recommendations or guided experiences
   - locals and travelers alike
2. Guides and experts:
   - locals with context
   - niche enthusiasts
   - professionals offering coaching or guided sessions

## 6. Product Outcomes
1. Faster path from question to trusted answer.
2. Higher-quality real-time guidance than static search/listings.
3. Reliable conversion from chat to booked experiences.
4. Sustainable earnings and retention for guides.

## 7. Success Metrics
1. Conversation metrics:
   - chats started per active user
   - response speed
   - conversation quality score
2. Conversion metrics:
   - chat -> offer
   - offer -> booking
3. Retention metrics:
   - repeat explorer usage
   - guide retention
4. Trust metrics:
   - review quality
   - incident/report rate

## 8. Strategic Product Scope
1. Chat and matching
2. Onboarding and role identity
3. Offer creation from chat
4. Booking and payment rails
5. Reviews, trust signals, moderation

## 9. Non-Goals (Current Stage)
1. Generic social networking feed
2. Broad marketplace expansion without trust controls
3. Feature breadth that weakens core chat experience

## 10. Decision Filter
Before implementation, answer:
1. Does this strengthen chat as the primary interface?
2. Does this improve access to real local/expert insight?
3. Does this increase trust and authenticity?
4. Does this improve conversion from conversation to experience?
5. Is the KPI impact clear?
